## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single isolated 2D pixel art game sprite for an animated Portuguese landing page telling the story of Kooizy's succulent. Primary request: a charming green rosette succulent, like an echeveria, in a small terracotta flower pot. The succulent is the sole character and subject. Classic 16-bit cozy garden RPG pixel art, deliberately large clean square pixel clusters, hard stepped edges, limited palette of deep forest green, jade, mint, warm terracotta and cream highlights. Compact, slightly three-quarter front view; overlapping thick fleshy leaves forming a clear rosette; tiny friendly face with two black pixel eyes and a small smile on the pot. Entire pot and leaves visible, generous transparent margin, centered, no floor or scenery. Genuinely transparent alpha background. No text, no lettering, no logos, no other objects, no soft edges, no gradients, no blur, no glow, no realistic texture, no vector illustration, no shadow outside the sprite. The image will be moved and gently bobbed with step-based web animations. Generate a single sprite, not a sprite sheet.
